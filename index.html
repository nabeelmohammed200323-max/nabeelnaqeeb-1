Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Which payment methods and Turkish banks are supported? ↓
Senders in Tanzania can deposit using Vodacom M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Halopesa, or local bank wire (CRDB, NMB). Payouts in Turkey are dispatched directly via Turkish Central Bank FAST rails to any bank IBAN (including VakıfBank, Ziraat Bankası, Türkiye İş Bankası, Garanti BBVA) in under 30 minutes, or collected in cash at our Istanbul branch desks (Pendik & Fatih).
What does "Upfront Fee Breakdown" mean? ↓
Unlike other brokers who deduct fees secretly during currency conversion or let intermediate Turkish correspondent banks shave off 50 to 150 TRY, Nabeel Naqeeb Remit displays an exact, itemized financial summary prior to confirmation. You see the principal, the exact 4% customs clearance fee and the exact guaranteed net amount the recipient will receive in Turkey.
What is the 4% customs charge and why is it required? ↓
The 4% customs fee is the regulatory and customs processing charge required for cross-border currency transfers and commercial consignments entering the Turkish financial framework from East Africa. It guarantees that funds arrive clean, declared, and protected against local banking freezes or tax queries.
How fast does the recipient receive money in Turkey? ↓
Using our FAST Instant mode, transfers to Turkish bank accounts (e.g. VakıfBank, Ziraat Bankası, etc.) typically land in under 20 to 30 minutes after M-Pesa or bank receipt confirmation. Cash pickups at our Istanbul partner centers are available same-day during business hours.
How does the 15-minute exchange rate lock protect me? ↓
When you calculate your transfer, our real-time exchange rate engine locks the interbank FX quote for a full 15 minutes with 0% currency spread margin. This completely protects your recipient from adverse exchange rate fluctuations while you authorize your payment from Tanzania.
